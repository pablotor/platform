# Introduction

The PabloTor Platform aims to be more of a recipe book than a boilerplate.

Many things in here won’t work for your specific project. But you will find many implementations ready to go that you can port to your own platform easily. While documentation usually covers very simple examples, here you will find an E2E scenario with all moving parts in place.

The platform is currently in active development, and many missing functionalities are coming further down the road.
